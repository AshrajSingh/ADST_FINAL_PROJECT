MCA Final Year Capstone Viva Defense Script

Speak naturally using these four structured answers when facing the external examiner.

Examiner Ready
1 "Why did you choose this dataset and how did you handle missing data?"
Your Answer:
"Sir/Madam, I selected this dataset because automated resume screening is a prime real-world use case of Business Intelligence in Human Resources. The dataset contains 1,000 candidate records with 10 key features covering experience, degree, projects, certifications, and expected salary.

In Power Query, I handled missing data in the Certifications column by using the Replace Values transformation to replace all null values with 'None'. Additionally, I added conditional columns to bucket experience into three meaningful tiers: Entry Level (0-2 Yrs), Mid Level (3-4 Yrs), and Senior Level (5+ Yrs), as well as a binary Hired Flag for fast mathematical processing."
2 "Can you explain your DAX measures? Why did you use CALCULATE and DIVIDE?"
Your Answer:
"Yes, Sir/Madam. I wrote explicit DAX measures instead of relying on default aggregations:
  • For total candidate volume, I used COUNTROWS which simply counts rows in the table.
  • For conditional measures like Total Hired and Total Rejected, I used CALCULATE. In DAX, CALCULATE is fundamental because it overrides and alters the filter context—here, filtering the dataset to only count rows where Recruiter Decision = 'Hire'.
  • For Hiring Rate %, I used DIVIDE([Total Hired], [Total Applicants], 0). I specifically chose the DIVIDE function over the standard forward-slash / because DIVIDE provides safe mathematical division—if the denominator is zero, it returns 0 instead of throwing a divide-by-zero error on our visual cards."
3 "What are the top 3 business insights discovered from your dashboard?"
Your Answer:
"From my visual analysis, three major findings emerged:
  1. The 5+ Year Seniority Ceiling: Candidates with 5 or more years of experience achieved a 100.0% hiring rate (442 out of 442 hired). 84.6% of all rejections occurred in the 0–2 years Entry Level bracket.
  2. Projects Compensate for Lack of Experience: Junior candidates (1-3 years experience) who completed 7 or more portfolio projects achieved a 100% hiring rate. Practical projects act as proof of competence.
  3. Salary Expectation Does Not Cause Rejections: The average salary requested by hired candidates ($80,483) is almost identical to rejected candidates ($77,884)—a difference of only 3.3%. This demonstrates that candidate selection was merit-based rather than cost-driven."
4 "In 30 seconds, what is the main takeaway of your Power BI dashboard?"
Your 30-Second Elevator Pitch:
"The main takeaway of my project is that modern hiring is shifting toward demonstrated practical capability. While seniority guarantees an interview, early-career candidates can overcome low work tenure by building strong technical project portfolios (7+ projects). My Power BI dashboard gives HR leaders transparent visibility into these screening patterns to prevent false rejections and hire high-potential talent faster."
